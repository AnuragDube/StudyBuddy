Data Structures and Algorithms — CS201
Unit I — Complexity and Linear Structures: Asymptotic analysis, Big-O notation, recurrence relations, Master theorem, arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues.
Unit II — Trees and Hashing: Binary search trees, AVL trees, Red-Black trees, B-Trees, heaps, priority queues, hash tables and collision resolution.
Unit III — Graph Algorithms: BFS, DFS, topological sort, Dijkstra's shortest path, Bellman-Ford, Kruskal and Prim minimum spanning trees.
Unit IV — Algorithm Design Paradigms: Divide and conquer, greedy algorithms, dynamic programming, backtracking, NP-completeness.
